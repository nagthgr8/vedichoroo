Krishna Mohan ("Bujji")
Born: 10 July 1974, 4:30AM   |   Missing since: 10 July 1996 (22 years old)
Birth Star: Purvabhadra 1st Pada
This page is dedicated to the loving memory and hope for my dear brother, Bujji, whose presence is missed every single day.
In loving memory of the times my dear brother Krishna Mohan (Bujji) and I shared during our younger days. Although Bujji is no longer with me, the memories we created together will forever be cherished and remembered.

Bujji was more than just a brother to me; he was a friend, a confidant, and a constant source of support and encouragement. I recall the times we spent together during our college days, and our movie nights were a source of great relief and joy for me. Bujji always showed me a great deal of respect and love, even during our most challenging moments.

Our childhood was not easy—after losing our parents, we were raised by our loving aunt and uncle. Though they gave us care and kindness, the absence of our parents left a mark, especially on Bujji. He was a gentle soul, but life’s hardships, heartbreak, and a traumatic incident changed him deeply. On his 22nd birthday, he left home, and since then, we have had no trace of his whereabouts.

In his last days at home, Bujji found solace and strength in his devotion to Shiridi Sai Baba. I remember seeing him pray with hope and faith, seeking peace and guidance.

"Bujji, wherever you are, I hope you have found peace and kindness. You are always in my prayers and thoughts. The memories we created—our laughter, our struggles, our dreams—remain alive in my heart."

"Why fear when I am here?"
— Shiridi Sai Baba

Although Bujji has left home, I know that he is still alive somewhere out there, and I hope he is doing well. This mobile app is dedicated to the memories we created together, as a testament to the bond we shared and the love that will always remain.

Wherever you are, dear brother, you hold a special place in my heart, and I will always treasure the memories we created together. May you find happiness and fulfillment in your life's journey, and may our paths cross once again someday. May Sai Baba guide and protect you, wherever you may be. Our love and hope for your return will never fade.
With love, your brother
